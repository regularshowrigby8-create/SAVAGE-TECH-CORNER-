What the agent and reviewer still handle

Meaning and priority

Reproduce failures, determine real root causes, preserve aliases, separate false positives from risks, and choose the next bounded ticket.

Correct repairs

Design changes, author behavior assertions, review patches, resolve dependency/API migrations and verify durable state. No blind bulk autofix.

Native reality

Device/WebView bridges, OEM behavior, physical performance, TalkBack usability and visually approved reference screens need native evidence.

Security and release

Interpret secret candidates without publishing values; decide trust boundaries and consent. Maintainers approve keys, protected reviews and production signing.

Measurement

Record at least ten comparable before/after active-effort samples. CI elapsed time or tool counts cannot establish 70–80% savings.

Unparsed evidence

Eight report formats have location parsers. Unsupported reports and malformed evidence remain explicit review work, never empty green results.
